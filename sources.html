THE SMALL PRINT

Sources & Credits.

References, authorship, image rights and how your journal data is stored.

Film information

The archive contains 129 films. The initial selection was checked against Criterion’s catalogue; the expanded selection includes the owner’s requested films and eight further director introductions. Titles, years and director names were checked against introductory text in the linked Wikipedia reference articles. Each film page carries a reference link.

Film synopses and style observations are original AI-assisted editorial prose. Director biographies are adapted excerpts from Wikipedia, attributed on each profile under CC BY-SA 4.0, with links to the source and licence. Genres and displayed production countries are concise classifications, rather than complete lists of international co-production partners. Director filmographies contain only films in this archive.

Film posters and director photographs

Every film has a sourced poster and every director profile has sourced photographic imagery. Filmmaking partnerships are identified accurately: Powell and Pressburger have separate portraits, and the Wachowskis use a joint photograph. Photographs and posters are shown without the previous visual effects.

Full image credits, creators, source links and rights records are available for every imported image. Posters and some promotional portraits are copyrighted reference images, not freely licensed assets. Creative Commons and public-domain material is identified by its source record. Resized Creative Commons images retain the source licence, including ShareAlike where applicable. No blanket rights clearance is claimed.

Current presentation

The website uses a shared black-and-white typographic design with orange-red accents. Anton and Roboto Condensed fonts are hosted locally under the included Open Font License. Film photographs are attributed in the image credits; original SVG cinema stickers decorate the About page. The homepage includes colour and monochrome photo controls. No generated film imagery is used.

Editorial authorship

All three articles are original AI-assisted editorial text, attributed to FRAME No. 1. They are not presented as the student’s own writing or as quotations from an outside critic.

Your journal and privacy

Watched status, favourites, watchlist, ratings and notes stay in this browser’s LocalStorage. No account, analytics, tracking script or server receives these entries. Clearing browser data removes them. Export a backup from My Frames to keep or transfer your journal. Backups may contain private notes.

Technology

This site uses HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. Its film dataset and images are local files; no API or remote image service is called while browsing. No private API token is embedded. Image retrieval was a development step, not a live website dependency. This project is not endorsed by its reference sources.

Accessibility

Keyboard navigation, labelled controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support and responsive layouts are included. Search, checklists and the personal journal need JavaScript. Film pages, editorial text and navigation remain readable without it.
