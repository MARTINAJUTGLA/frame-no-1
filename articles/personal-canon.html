ARTICLES / A PRACTICAL FILM GUIDE

How to start a film journal

A practical way to record what you watch without turning it into homework.

By FRAME No. 1 · AI-assisted sample article · 19 November 2026 · 2 min read

Sample article written with ChatGPT/Codex assistance for this university project. Viewing observations are editorial interpretation.

Start with one film

You do not need to watch a certain number of classics before keeping a film journal. Start with the last film you watched. Find it in the archive and mark it as watched. If you would like to see another film later, add it to your watchlist. The two lists serve different purposes: one records what you have seen, and the other helps you choose what to watch next.

Write one useful note

A note can be as short as a sentence. Record a scene you enjoyed, a performance you noticed or a question you still have. Ratings are optional, and they represent your own opinion. You can enjoy a film without giving it a score. Favourites are a separate way to keep films you would like to return to.

Use checklists at your own pace

Try a director or genre checklist when you want a little direction. You can also create a personal list and enter films that are not yet in the archive. Print that checklist or save it as a PDF if you prefer a paper copy. Your journal is stored in this browser, so export a backup before clearing browser data or moving to another device.
