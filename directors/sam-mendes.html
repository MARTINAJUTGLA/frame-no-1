About Sam Mendes

Sir Samuel Alexander Mendes (born 1 August 1965) is an English filmmaker and stage director. Mendes was appointed a CBE for his services to drama in 2000, and he was knighted in 2020 by Queen Elizabeth II. Known for his directorial work on stage and screen, he has received various accolades including an Academy Award, five BAFTA Awards, two Golden Globe Awards, three Olivier Awards and two Tony Awards.
