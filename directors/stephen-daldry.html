About Stephen Daldry

Stephen David Daldry CBE (born 2 May 1960) is an English director and producer of film, theatre, and television. His accolades include two BAFTA Awards, two Emmy Awards, a Laurence Olivier Award and three Tony Awards as well as nominations for three Academy Awards and two Golden Globe Awards. He was made a Commander of the Order of the British Empire (CBE) by Queen Elizabeth II in 2003.
