About Greta Gerwig

Greta Celeste Gerwig ( GUR-wig; born August 4, 1983) is an American actress and filmmaker. She was initially known for working on various mumblecore films, such as Baghead (2008) and Yeast (2008), in which she became an important figure in the movement. Since then she has expanded from acting in and co-writing independent films to directing major studio films.
